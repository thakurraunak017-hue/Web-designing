Content: This is the innermost area where the actual content of the element resides, such as text, images, or other media. Its dimensions are determined by the width and height properties. Padding: This is the transparent space that surrounds the content, acting as a buffer between the content and the element's border. Padding adds internal spacing within the element and is controlled by the padding properties (e.g., padding-top, padding-right, padding-bottom, padding-left, or the shorthand padding).
